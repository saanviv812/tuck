# changelog

## 0.1.0 (first release)

the first version of tuck.

- press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `Space` anywhere to open the popup, type, press Enter
- drafts are kept if you close the popup by accident
- pockets (optional), #tags, and a library to search and sort everything later
- little suggestions (kind, tags, pocket) you can accept or ignore, all worked out on your computer
- nine pixel buddies whose eyes follow your cursor. the whole app takes your buddy's colour
- a desk buddy that hangs out on your taskbar, with a right-click menu
- settings that preview until you press save changes
- tray icon and menu, start when you log in, export as markdown
- quiet, signed auto-updates
- your data is one file on your computer, saved safely with a backup copy
